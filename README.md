## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineering student at PUC Minas** and a Full-Stack Developer with a strong interest in **back-end development and software architecture**.

I currently work with software development and maintenance, gaining hands-on experience with real-world systems.

One of my recent projects involved migrating a **municipal NFS-e (Electronic Service Invoice) system to Brazil's national standard**, working with:

* XML generation and validation
* Digital document signing
* DANFSE generation
* Database modeling and changes
* Taxation and fiscal rules
* Integration with the national NFS-e standard

I also work with **PHP and Scriptcase** for the development and maintenance of business systems.

I'm continuously improving my skills and looking for opportunities to build software that solves real problems.
